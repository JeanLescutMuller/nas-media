# nas-media

**Every TV show in Plex or on your Plex Watchlist, and every movie on your Watchlist, downloads by itself on a home NAS, a few episodes ahead of what you watched. Everything you do happens in Plex.**

| You do, in Plex | It does |
|---|---|
| add a show or a movie to your Watchlist | downloads it (a show from S01E01, or after the last episode marked watched) |
| just watch | the next episodes are always there |
| add a file to the list "Bad files" | deletes it and downloads another release |

Built on Sonarr, Radarr, Prowlarr and qBittorrent. The full README, with diagrams: [README.html](https://htmlpreview.github.io/?https://github.com/JeanLescutMuller/nas-media/blob/main/README.html) · rules for agents: [AGENTS.md](AGENTS.md)
