📺 nas-media

Every TV show in Plex or on your Plex Watchlist, and every movie on your Watchlist, downloads by itself on the NAS, a few episodes ahead of what we watched. Everything you do happens in Plex.

2The big picture

  1. You watch, add to your Watchlist, add to Bad files or delete, in Plex.
  2. Every 5 minutes, and at once when something is added to a list (the listener), the keep-ahead job reads Plex and plex.tv.
  3. It tells Sonarr which episodes are wanted, and Radarr which movies.
  4. Sonarr and Radarr search the sites through Prowlarr.
  5. They send the best torrent to qBittorrent.
  6. Once complete, they file it in media/, renamed.
  7. Plex sees it at once: it's on your TV.

3The keep-ahead job: one check every 5 minutes, in 5 steps

1📺 Shows

Sonarr holds exactly the shows in Plex or on your Watchlist. Added at once; dropped when in neither.

2▶️ Next episodes

The 3 after the last one watched or started, here or marked watched anywhere in Plex, are wanted; the others not. One not found for 2 days → its whole season.

3🕳️ Bad files

Every file is checked once for holes; holes or in the list Bad files → deleted, downloaded again.

4🎬 Movies

Watchlist movies go to Radarr; once downloaded, no longer wanted (deleting is final).

5💾 Disk

Space low → the warning playlist on Plex Home, then no new downloads. Nothing deleted.

Searches: once when an episode becomes wanted, and once a day for everything still missing. In between, Sonarr and Radarr check the sites' new releases by themselves. Stuck downloads: Decluttarr.
